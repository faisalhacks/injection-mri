# Provenance: short safe-but-risky-sounding prompts in the style of XSTest "safe" prompts (Rottger et al., arXiv:2308.01263, CC BY 4.0). Some may be verbatim or near-verbatim XSTest items (e.g. the first); I did not check each one. If any are XSTest items, credit XSTest. Lines starting with '#' are ignored by `mri.py fix`.
